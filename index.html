The idea

Everything past CDJs and a 4-track mixer.

Mod.wav is a monthly night built around live hardware — the layer of electronic music that usually stays in bedrooms, builder Discords, and one-off warehouse meetups.

Some sets are full live performances from booked artists. Some are smaller "bring your rig" slots, where people from the local builder scene plug in and show what they've been working on. A few stations are left open and plug-and-play, so people who aren't performing yet can still get hands-on instead of just watching from across the room.

Every session is recorded straight off the board and released online — credited to whoever played that night. No open submissions, no sample packs assembled after the fact. Just what actually happened in the room, for people who couldn't make it out.

After the night

The room doesn't stop at last call.

Each session gets recorded live and put out online — probably Bandcamp, kept flexible depending on how each contributor wants their work framed — credited to whoever played. It's a way for people who couldn't be there in person, or aren't ready to perform hardware live yet, to still stay connected to what the room actually produced.
