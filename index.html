The concept

Everything past CDJs and a 4-track mixer.

The Bay Area has a real synth/modular/DIY scene — builder Discords, meetups, a steady stream of Eurorack and circuit-bending work — but no regular room for it.

Mod.wav is that room. Booked live sets, bring-your-rig slots, and open plug-and-play stations. A space to build, share, and play sound together.
